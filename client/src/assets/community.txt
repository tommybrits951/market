COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

Welcome to [SITE NAME]!

Our community brings together people who want to connect, share content, communicate, discover products, and participate in a marketplace.

These Community Guidelines establish expectations for everyone using our Platform.

BE RESPECTFUL

Treat all users with respect.

We do not allow:

Harassment or bullying.
Threats or intimidation.
Hate speech or discriminatory attacks.
Targeted abuse.
Repeated unwanted contact.
Encouraging violence against others.
NO IMPERSONATION

Users may not:

Pretend to be another person.
Create fake official accounts.
Misrepresent their identity to deceive others.
Use another person's photographs without authorization to impersonate them.
Falsely claim affiliation with a business or organization.
NO SPAM

The following activities are prohibited:

Repetitive unwanted messages.
Automated engagement manipulation.
Fake followers or interactions.
Mass unsolicited advertising.
Misleading promotional campaigns.
Repeated duplicate posts.
CONTENT STANDARDS

Content published on [SITE NAME] must comply with applicable laws and these Guidelines.

Prohibited content includes:

Illegal material.
Threats of violence.
Non-consensual intimate imagery.
Exploitative content involving minors.
Content promoting fraud.
Malicious software.
Doxxing and unauthorized disclosure of sensitive personal information.
Content that infringes intellectual property rights.
MISINFORMATION AND DECEPTION

Users must not intentionally deceive others through:

Fake product advertisements.
Fraudulent reviews.
False claims about products or services.
Fake giveaways.
Misleading financial offers.
Manipulated marketplace listings.
PRIVATE MESSAGES

Private messaging must be used responsibly.

Users may not use messages to:

Threaten or harass others.
Distribute scams.
Send malicious links or files.
Request sensitive information under false pretenses.
Repeatedly contact users who have asked them to stop.
MARKETPLACE BEHAVIOR

Buyers and sellers must act honestly.

Sellers must:

Describe products accurately.
Use genuine product images or clearly disclose illustrative images.
Disclose known defects.
Honor agreed transaction terms.
Avoid fraudulent listings.

Buyers must:

Provide accurate transaction information.
Avoid fraudulent payment disputes.
Communicate respectfully.
Follow applicable purchasing rules.
REPORTING VIOLATIONS

Users can report:

Accounts.
Posts.
Comments.
Messages.
Marketplace listings.
Suspicious activity.

Reports should contain accurate information and should not be used to harass or falsely accuse others.

ENFORCEMENT

We may take action when these Guidelines are violated.

Possible actions include:

Removing content.
Issuing warnings.
Restricting posting or messaging.
Removing marketplace listings.
Temporarily suspending accounts.
Permanently terminating accounts.
Reporting illegal conduct to appropriate authorities.

Enforcement decisions may consider the severity, frequency, and context of violations.
